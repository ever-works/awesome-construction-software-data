## Overview

OpenConstructionERP is an open-source construction ERP system covering BOQ, PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff, and AI cost matching. It includes 293 guided cases, 120,000+ priced positions, support for 44 languages, 190+ modules, and cost data for 40+ markets.

## Features

- BOQ (Bill of Quantities) management
- PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff
- AI cost matching
- 293 guided cases
- 120,000+ priced positions
- 44 languages
- 190+ modules
- Cost data for 40+ markets
- pip installable

## Pricing

Free and open-source under AGPL-3.0 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted, Python package installable via pip.

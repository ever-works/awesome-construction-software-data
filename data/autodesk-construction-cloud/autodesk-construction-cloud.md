## Overview

Autodesk Construction Cloud is a connected construction platform that unifies design, planning, project management, and field operations through cloud-based BIM and construction management tools.

## Features

- Document management and control
- Project cost and field management
- Design collaboration and BIM coordination
- Quality and safety management
- Bid management and trade partner collaboration
- Analytics and predictive insights
- Integration with Autodesk Revit, Navisworks, and AutoCAD

## Pricing

Paid enterprise software with tiered pricing based on project volume and user count.

## Overview

FreeCAD is a free and open-source multiplatform 3D parametric modeler. It is designed primarily for mechanical engineering and product design but extends to architectural and BIM workflows through its modular workbench system.

## Features

- Parametric modeling with history-based design
- Modular architecture with workbenches for different domains
- Python scripting API for automation and extension
- Cross-platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
- BIM workbench for architectural modeling and IFC support
- Mesh, FEM, and drafting workbenches

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GNU LGPL license. Source code available at https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD

## Deployment

Desktop application (self-hosted, locally installed).

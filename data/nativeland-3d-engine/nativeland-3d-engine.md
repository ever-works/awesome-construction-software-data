## Overview

A free and open-source 3D engine based on Vue3 + THREE.JS with its accompanying editor. It features functions such as BIM lightweighting, CAD parsing preview, particle system, and plugin system.

## Features

- BIM lightweighting
- CAD parsing preview
- Particle system
- Plugin system
- Vue3 + THREE.JS based
- Accompanying editor

## Pricing

Free and open-source. Repository: https://github.com/nativeland/nativeland

## Deployment

Web-based.

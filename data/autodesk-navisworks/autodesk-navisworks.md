## Overview

Autodesk Navisworks is a BIM project review software for 3D model coordination, clash detection, and construction simulation, enabling multidisciplinary design review and conflict resolution before construction begins.

## Features

- 3D model aggregation from multiple BIM formats
- Clash detection and interference checking
- 4D construction simulation and sequencing
- 5D cost estimation and quantification
- Model review and navigation
- Timeliner for construction sequencing
- Integration with Revit, AutoCAD, and AutoCAD MEP

## Pricing

Paid commercial software with subscription licensing through Autodesk.

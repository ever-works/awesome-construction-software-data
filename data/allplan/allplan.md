## Overview

Allplan is a BIM and CAD software solution developed by Nemetschek for architects and engineers, supporting 3D modeling, 2D drafting, and construction documentation throughout the building lifecycle.

## Features

- 3D BIM modeling and 2D drafting
- Architectural and structural engineering tools
- Reinforced concrete design
- Quantity takeoff and cost estimation
- IFC support for interoperability
- Collaboration tools for multidisciplinary teams

## Pricing

Paid commercial software with licensing options available through Nemetschek.

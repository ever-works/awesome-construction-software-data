## Overview

Knowify is an all-in-one construction management solution designed for trade contractors and remodelers. It provides real-time business insights with an easy-to-set-up platform featuring a clean user interface.

## Features

- Project management for trade contractors
- Real-time business insights
- Client communication tools
- Financial management
- Integrations with other business tools
- Easy setup and clean UI

## Pricing

Paid with free trial available. Essentials plan starts at $149 per month.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).

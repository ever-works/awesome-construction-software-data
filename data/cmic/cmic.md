## Overview

CMiC is cloud-based, single database project management and financials software designed for construction companies. It provides an integrated platform with scalability, real-time data access, and mobile access for field and office teams.

## Features

- Integrated project management and financials
- Single database architecture
- Scalability for growing companies
- Real-time data access
- Mobile access for field and office teams
- Accounting and ERP capabilities

## Pricing

Paid enterprise subscription with tiered pricing based on company size and modules.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).

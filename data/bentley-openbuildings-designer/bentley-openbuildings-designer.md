## Overview

Bentley OpenBuildings Designer is a BIM software for architectural design, engineering, and construction documentation, supporting multidisciplinary building design and analysis workflows.

## Features

- Architectural design and documentation
- Structural analysis and design
- MEP systems design
- Energy analysis and simulation
- IFC support for interoperability
- Generative design capabilities
- Cloud collaboration through ProjectWise

## Pricing

Paid commercial software with subscription licensing through Bentley Systems.

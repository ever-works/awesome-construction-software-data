## Overview

STACK is a cloud-based construction estimating and takeoff software for contractors, enabling digital quantity takeoff, cost estimating, and bid management from construction plans and specifications.

## Features

- Digital quantity takeoff from PDF plans
- Cost estimating and material pricing
- Bid management and proposal generation
- Custom assemblies and databases
- Cloud-based collaboration
- Integration with accounting and project management tools
- Mobile app for field access

## Pricing

Paid subscription software with tiered plans; free trial available.

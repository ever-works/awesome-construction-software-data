## Overview

Autodesk BIM 360 is construction project management software that provides cloud-based collaboration, document management, and issue tracking for architecture, engineering, and construction teams. It is part of the Autodesk Construction Cloud ecosystem.

## Features

- Cloud-based document management
- Team collaboration tools
- Issue tracking and resolution
- Model coordination
- Field management
- Project analytics

## Pricing

Paid enterprise subscription with tiered pricing based on users and features.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).

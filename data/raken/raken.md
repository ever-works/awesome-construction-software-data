## Overview

Raken is a field management and daily reporting app for construction teams, enabling project documentation, time tracking, photo capture, and communication from the job site through mobile and web platforms.

## Features

- Daily field reports and daily logs
- Time tracking and crew management
- Photo and video documentation
- Toolbox talks and safety meetings
- RFIs and issue tracking
- Integration with Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, and other construction platforms
- Offline capability for field use

## Pricing

Paid subscription software with tiered plans based on project volume and users.

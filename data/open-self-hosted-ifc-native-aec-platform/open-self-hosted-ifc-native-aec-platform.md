## Overview

Open, self-hosted, IFC-native AEC platform combining web BIM viewer and modeling with a ~100-module GC portal covering RFIs, pay applications, CPM scheduling, construction accounting (double-entry GL/WIP → QuickBooks), e-signatures, AI assist, IDS authoring, estimating with embodied carbon, development proforma, and operate/dispose capabilities (CMMS, facility condition, rent roll, waterfall).

## Features

- Web BIM viewer + modeling
- IFC-native
- Self-hosted
- ~100-module GC portal
- RFIs, pay apps, CPM scheduling
- Construction accounting: double-entry GL/WIP → QuickBooks
- E-signatures
- AI assist
- IDS authoring
- Estimating + embodied carbon
- Development proforma
- CMMS, facility condition, rent roll, waterfall
